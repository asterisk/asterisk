# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community. Participation must be free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, precise, and patient.
- Critique code and decisions, never people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, call content, private configuration, or security-sensitive details.
- Give factual credit and welcome corrections.
- Prefer reproducible evidence over speculation, especially for safety and security claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Security reports must not be weaponized or disclosed publicly before maintainers have a reasonable opportunity to respond.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban. Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and be documented privately.

## Reporting

Use the repository's private security-reporting route for security concerns and sensitive conduct reports. Do not include secrets or private data in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical, with confidentiality preserved to the extent possible.
